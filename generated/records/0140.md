## G161. A fixed replacement budget leaves a large actual residual

Fix an ordinary character chi modulo a fixed q. The arithmetic source is
`a(n)=mu(n)chi(n)`, with squareful and nonunit zeros and n=1 retained.
It is distinct from the Eisenstein native-H coefficient system below.

Suppose every allowed edge {n,m}, n!=m, obeys
`|n-m|<=C min(n,m)^theta`, where `C>0`, `0<=theta<1`, and at each
squarefree endpoint at most a fixed d>=1 of its prime factors are deleted
when moving to the other endpoint. The restriction applies independently
at each squarefree endpoint, regardless of flow orientation. Auxiliary
squareful/nonunit integer vertices are permitted under this same rule.

Let g=gcd(n,m). Since g divides n-m,

    g<=|n-m|,       n/g >= C^(-1)n^(1-theta).

`n/g<=y^d`. Thus `n^(1-theta)>C y^d` makes n isolated in the entire graph,
